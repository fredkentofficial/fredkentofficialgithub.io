Thank you for your interest in joining our Computer Science programs. We are committed to providing quality education to prepare you for a successful career in technology.

Requirements

To be eligible for admission, applicants must:

  1. Have completed secondary level (or equivalent)
  2. Possess basic knowledge of English and Mathematics
  3. Show interest in technology and computing
  4. Submit all required documents
Required documents
  • Academic certificates
  • Copy of national ID/passport
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Completed application form
